Dear Editors,

I am pleased to submit the enclosed manuscript, "Component NMA: A Browser-Based Tool for Component Network Meta-Analysis of Complex Interventions," for consideration as a Software Article in Research Synthesis Methods.

Component network meta-analysis is increasingly important for evaluating complex, multi-component interventions, yet the methodology remains accessible only through the R netmeta package, limiting adoption among researchers without R programming skills. This manuscript presents the first browser-based component NMA tool, implementing the additive model of Welton et al. (2009) and Rucker et al. (2020) with DerSimonian-Laird random-effects estimation, optional pairwise interaction terms, and comprehensive visualization (network graph, forest plot, design matrix).

The tool requires no installation, runs entirely in the user's browser, and generates R-equivalent code for cross-validation with netmeta::netcomb. It is freely available at https://github.com/mahmood726-cyber/component-nma and validated by 25 automated tests.

The manuscript has not been published or submitted elsewhere. There are no competing interests. No external funding was received.
